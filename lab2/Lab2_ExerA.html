About the Event

Faculty Career Day 2026 is an event organized to help students explore career Opportunities, develop professional skills, and connect with industry professionals.

Guest Speaker: Software Engineer

An experienced Software engineer will share insight about the technology industry, career pathways, and the skills required to become a successful developer.

Career Development Workshop

Student will learn how to prepare a professional resume, improve interview skills, and prepare for their future careers.
